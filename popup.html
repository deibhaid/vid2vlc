Vid2VLC

Stream web videos to VLC Media Player

📺 How to use: Right-click on any video or page with videos:
  • "Add Stream to VLC" - Download playlist for single video
  • "Add All Videos to VLC Playlist" - Download playlist with all videos on page
🎬 Quick Start:
  1. Right-click on a video or page
  2. Select a Vid2VLC option
  3. Open the downloaded .m3u file with VLC
  4. Enjoy streaming!
✨ Features:
  • Works on all platforms (Linux, macOS, Windows)
  • No configuration required
  • Detects multiple video formats
  • Creates standard M3U playlists
